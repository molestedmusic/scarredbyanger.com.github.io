OVERVIEW

Scarred By Anger is a Canadian heavy metal band formed in Vernon, British Columbia, by Art Carriere and Dave Marlin in 1998. Their only known release is the demo EP Bludgeon, recorded at Black Lab Recordings in Vernon by local home-recording engineer Harvey Lebrash. The modest basement studio served small bands making demos and provided recording services only, leaving mixing and mastering to others.

Bludgeon was intended for release in 1999, but severe technical limitations and inexpensive equipment left the initial mixes unfinished and badly degraded. The EP remained unreleased and was effectively lost for 25 years. In 2024, the only surviving multitrack recording was recovered from a Digital Audio Tape (DAT) recorded at the unusual sample rate of 22.05 kHz. The EP was then remixed and remastered using modern technology, bringing it to the best quality the surviving material could support. Its low sample rate still gives the recording a dark, degraded sound that complements the EP’s bleak atmosphere and abrasive musical style.
